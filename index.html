Questions

Asked before every first visit.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Elective visits are by appointment, between 11:00 AM and 9:30 PM — call 0335 1480480 beforehand. Dental emergencies are taken on call, 24 hours, including Sundays.

What happens at a first check-up?

An examination of your teeth and gums, with X-rays where they are needed. The dentist explains what is found and what the options are, in plain language, before any treatment is agreed.

Does a root canal hurt?

Patients here describe it as smooth, and as painless as possible. Kousar Jelani's Google review of her scaling and root canal says the dentist “carefully explained both procedures, addressing all my concerns and ensuring I was comfortable throughout.”

When should a wisdom tooth come out?

If it hurts, crowds the teeth beside it, or keeps getting infected, it is worth an assessment — the clinic examines the tooth and the X-ray first, then advises. Wisdom tooth extraction is one of the clinic's surgical services.

Do you accept card payments?

Yes — VISA is listed as accepted on the clinic's Google profile.

Where exactly is the clinic?

87-H, Block H Commercial, Phase 1, DHA, Lahore — on the commercial strip in Block H. The map above, or the Google Maps link, will take you to the door.
